How does a project with Anzato run?

In four steps: a free 30-minute call, a written scope with a fixed quote, a build you can watch on a staging link, then launch and handover. Most of what slows a first project down is paperwork and guessing. Here is the whole path, up front.

  1. A 30-minute call

    Bring the site or idea. You leave with a straight answer on what is holding it back, even if we never work together.

  2. Scope and quote

    A written scope with a fixed price and dates. NDA and agreement signed with a registered company.

  3. Build in the open

    A staging link from the first week and a short update every week. No disappearing for a month.

  4. Launch and hand over

    Hosting, analytics and Search Console in your name. Then a retainer if you want one, and no hard feelings if you don't.

Questions people ask before booking

How much does a project cost?

The starting prices are in the pricing section. The exact number depends on scope, which is why the first call is free. You get a fixed price in writing before any work starts, and it does not change unless the scope does.

Can you work on the site I already have?

Yes. Plenty of work is fixing, speeding up or extending an existing WordPress, Shopify or custom site rather than starting again. We will tell you honestly if a rebuild is cheaper.

I only need SEO. Do you do that on its own?

Yes. Technical SEO and AI search work is often where we start, beginning with an audit so you can see the problems before paying to fix them.

Can you get my business mentioned in ChatGPT and other AI answers?

We make your site easy for AI systems to crawl, read and quote: open crawler access, structured data, a clear answer at the top of each page and an llms.txt file. Then we track which AI tools send you visitors. Nobody can guarantee a mention, and we will not claim to.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with clients in the US, UAE, Singapore, the UK and India, over video calls and email, with working hours that overlap US mornings, the Gulf and Singapore.

What happens after launch?

You get everything: code, logins and a short handover. If you want ongoing work, a monthly retainer covers updates, SEO and reporting. If not, you leave with nothing tied to us.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks of a signed scope. Small fixes can often start sooner.

Do you use templates or page builders?

Not for client sites we build from scratch. We work in code, so pages stay fast and nothing breaks when a plugin updates. If your site already runs on a builder, we work with what is there.
